# AI Agents Vs Agentic AI

## AI Agents

AI agents referes to individual software programs designed to perform specific programs designed to perform specific tasks with a degress of autonomy.

1. **Functionality:** AI Agents handles specific tasks, it has limited autonomy.

2. **Descion Making:** Single entity with a focused purpose.

**Example**:

> **Customer service chatbot**: Answers basic questions


## Agentic AI

Agentic AI describes a broader framework where multiple AI agents can collaborate and make decisions independently to achieve a larger goal, emphasizing adaptablility and learning from experience rather than just following predefined rules.

1. **Functionality:** Agentic AI focuses on the overall system with the ability to learn, adapt and make complex decision.

2. **Descion Making:** Network of agents working together to achieve a goal.

**Example**: 

> **Smart Home System**
> 
> An AI Agentic system where there are different AI agents which maintain things like Lights, Thermostat, etc.., to reduce the electric usage.
