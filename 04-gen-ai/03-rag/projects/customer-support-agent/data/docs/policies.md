# Company Policies and Procedures

## Return and Refund Policy

### Return Window
- **Standard Returns**: within 30 days from ordered date
- **Extended Returns**: 60 days for items purchased during holiday season (November-December)
- **Defective Items**: 90 days for manufacturer defects

### Eligibility Requirements
- Item must be unused and in original condition with all packaging and accessories
- Original receipt or order confirmation required
- Items purchased on clearance are final sale unless defective

### Return Process
1. Initiate return through customer account portal or call support
2. Receive return shipping label via email
3. Ship item back to returns center within 14 days of approval
4. Refund issued 5-7 business days after item received and inspected

### Refund Methods
- Original payment method: Credited within 5-7 business days
- Store credit: Instant credit (10% bonus on credit amount)
- Gift card: Issued within 24 hours

### Non-Returnable Items
- Underwear and intimate apparel (for hygiene)
- Opened software or digital licenses
- Items explicitly marked as final sale
- Custom or personalized items (except defective)
- Items purchased over 30 days ago (except defects)

## Shipping Policy

### Standard Shipping
- **Delivery Time**: 5-7 business days
- **Cost**: Free for orders over $50, otherwise $5.99
- **Coverage**: Continental United States only

### Express Shipping
- **Delivery Time**: 2-3 business days
- **Cost**: $14.99
- **Guaranteed**: Refund if not delivered on time

### Priority/Overnight
- **Delivery Time**: Next business day
- **Cost**: $24.99
- **Available**: Monday-Friday orders only

### International Shipping
- **Available to**: Canada and Mexico
- **Delivery Time**: 10-14 business days
- **Cost**: Calculated at checkout based on weight and destination
- **Duties/Taxes**: Customer responsible for any customs fees

### Shipping Restrictions
- Hazardous materials require special handling (additional $15 fee)
- Oversized items (over 100 lbs) require white glove delivery
- Some items restricted to continental US only

## Product Warranty

### Standard Warranty
- **Coverage**: 1 year from purchase date
- **Includes**: Manufacturing defects, hardware failures
- **Excludes**: Accidental damage, wear and tear, improper use

### Extended Warranty
- **Available for**: Electronics, appliances (12+ months only)
- **Coverage**: Additional 2-5 years depending on product
- **Cost**: 10-25% of product price
- **Includes**: Accidental damage protection on select plans

### Making a Warranty Claim
1. Contact support with order number and issue description
2. Submit photos of defective item if requested
3. Company arranges replacement or repair
4. Customer ships defective item (return label provided)
5. Replacement ships within 5 business days of receiving defective item
