# Frequently Asked Questions

## Shipping & Delivery

**Q: How long does shipping take?**
A: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping takes 2-3 business days. Overnight delivery available for $24.99. Delivery times are from order placement, not payment processing.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
A: Yes, if the order hasn't shipped yet. Contact support immediately with your order number. If already shipped, you can refuse delivery and we'll reshop to correct address.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
A: We ship to Canada and Mexico with 10-14 day delivery. Other countries available through third-party services. Customer pays all duties and taxes.

**Q: What if my package arrives damaged?**
A: Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We'll either ship replacement immediately or process full refund. Damaged packaging doesn't require repayment of shipping.

**Q: Is there free shipping?**
A: Free standard shipping on orders over $50. All other orders pay $5.99 shipping fee.

## Returns & Refunds

**Q: How do I return an item?**
A: Log into your account, go to "Orders", select the order, and click "Return Item". Follow the prompts and print your return label. Ship within 14 days.

**Q: When will I get my refund?**
A: Refunds process 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect your return. Depends on payment method (credit cards may take longer).

**Q: Can I return items without a receipt?**
A: Yes, we can look up orders by email address or phone number.

**Q: What's your return policy on sale items?**
A: Sale items are final sale but returnable if defective. Refund at sale price, not original price.

**Q: Can I exchange items instead of returning?**
A: Yes, initiate exchange through returns portal. If size/color change is small, we can ship new item before receiving the return.

**Q: Why was my return rejected?**
A: Common reasons: Item used/damaged, outside return window, missing original packaging, clearance/final sale item. Contact support if you disagree.

## Payments & Billing

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), debit, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay.

**Q: Do you offer payment plans?**
A: Yes, for orders over $250: 3, 6, or 12-month interest-free plans available through Shop Pay.

**Q: Why was my payment declined?**
A: Common reasons: Card expired, insufficient funds, fraud block, or incorrect address. Contact your bank to verify, then try again. Contact support if persistent.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes, we use 256-bit encryption and are PCI Level 1 compliant. Your information is never stored in plaintext.

**Q: Can I use multiple payment methods for one order?**
A: No, one payment method per order. However, you can split across multiple orders if needed.

## Products & Orders

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Log into your account to see tracking number. You'll also receive tracking email with carrier link.

**Q: What if an item is out of stock?**
A: We'll notify you immediately. You can wait for restock (we'll hold your order) or cancel for full refund.

**Q: Do you have a warranty?**
A: Standard 1-year manufacturer's warranty on all electronics. Extended warranties available for 10-25% of product price.

**Q: Can I order out-of-stock items?**
A: Yes, you can pre-order. You'll be charged when item ships, and notified of expected delivery date.

**Q: How do I contact you about a defective item?**
A: Call support or submit claim through account portal. Have order number ready. We'll arrange replacement or repair under warranty.

## Account & Loyalty

**Q: How do I join your loyalty program?**
A: Account automatically created as Bronze member (free). Upgrade to Silver, Gold, or Platinum in account settings.

**Q: What benefits do loyalty members get?**
A: Discounts (5-15%), free/discounted shipping, early sale access, and priority support depending on tier.

**Q: Can I cancel my loyalty membership?**
A: Yes, cancel anytime through account settings. Unused annual fee will be refunded if within 30 days.

**Q: How do loyalty points work?**
A: $1 spent = 1 point. Redeem points for discounts (100 points = $5 credit). Points don't expire.

**Q: Can I transfer my account to someone else?**
A: No, accounts are non-transferable. Create new account for gift recipient and transfer order history if needed.
